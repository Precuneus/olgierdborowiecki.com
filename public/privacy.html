Privacy

This is my personal site. It sets no cookies, tracks nothing and stores nothing on your device. Everything on it loads from this address.

If you write to me, your message reaches me through Gmail. I keep it as long as we are in touch, because answering you is my legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) of the General Data Protection Regulation).

Write to me at olgierd.borowiecki@gmail.com to see, correct or delete what I hold about you. You can also complain to the Polish data protection office.
