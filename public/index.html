Researching

how the brain experiences

From concept cells to memory systems to brain networks, I search for theories explaining the absurdity of experiencing. I study how the brain creates the experience of its model of the world. From studies in mice to fMRI in humans, I want to understand how biological neural networks work during experience. I use games in my studies, because games are metaphors for everyday life.
